MouPilotSocial

by Mounalby

MouPilotSocial is a private content tool used by its owner to create short videos (quotes, educational and motion-graphic videos), schedule them, and publish them to the owner's own accounts on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, and Threads.

How it works

  • The owner prepares content (text, images) and the tool renders the videos.
  • Every video is reviewed and approved by the owner before it is published.
  • Approved videos are uploaded to the owner's connected account using the official platform APIs (for TikTok: Login Kit and Content Posting API).

The tool is not offered to the public and does not collect data from other users.

Bahasa Indonesia

MouPilotSocial adalah alat konten pribadi milik Mounalby untuk membuat video pendek, menjadwalkan, dan mengunggahnya ke akun TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, dan Threads milik pemilik sendiri. Setiap video diperiksa dan disetujui pemilik sebelum diunggah. Aplikasi ini tidak dibuka untuk umum.
