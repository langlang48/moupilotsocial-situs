Privacy Policy

MouPilotSocial by Mounalby · Last updated: 13 November 2026

MouPilotSocial ("the tool") is a private tool used by its owner to create, schedule, and publish content to the owner's own social media accounts. This policy explains what data the tool accesses and how it is handled.

Data the tool accesses

  • Basic profile information of the connected account: account ID, display name, username, and avatar.
  • Statistics of the connected account and its videos (for example followers, views, likes, comments, shares) to show analytics to the owner inside the tool.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos that the owner created and approved inside the tool.

How data is used

  • Only to publish the owner's own content and to display analytics to the owner.
  • The tool does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties and does not use it for advertising.
  • The tool does not access data of other TikTok users.

Storage and security

  • Access tokens are stored encrypted in the tool's private database on the owner's computer.
  • Videos and media are stored locally and are only sent to the platform the owner chose to publish to.

Removing access and deleting data

The owner can disconnect an account at any time from the tool's "Connected accounts" page, which deletes the stored tokens. Access can also be revoked from the platform's own settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions).

Changes

This policy may be updated when the tool changes. The date above shows the latest version.
